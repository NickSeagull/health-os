Reflects vitamin D stores. Skin synthesis depends on the angle of sunlight,
so levels predictably decline in winter at high latitudes [evidence level A].

Target ranges differ between guidelines: see [[source/vitamin-d-guideline]].
Units vary between laboratories: ng/mL and nmol/L differ by a factor of 2.5;
comparing without normalization produces a false trend.
